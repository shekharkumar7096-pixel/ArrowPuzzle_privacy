1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Arrow Puzzle handles user information. We respect your privacy and designed the game to run without requiring user accounts, logins, or developer-operated cloud databases.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Arrow Puzzle stores gameplay progress locally on your device using Android Room and Jetpack DataStore, including:

  • Completed levels, star ratings, best move counts, and daily challenge completions.
  • Virtual in-game coin balance, free hint balance, and unlocked visual themes.
  • Audio, vibration, and language preferences.

We do not collect, sell, or upload your personal files, contacts, photos, messages, or precise GPS location.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Arrow Puzzle integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display Banner Ads on the Home Screen, optional Rewarded Video Ads (e.g., to refill hearts or earn bonus hints/coins), and paced Interstitial Ads between completed levels.

When ads are requested or displayed, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain data—such as your device's Android Advertising ID (AD_ID), IP address (for approximate location and fraud detection), device type/OS version, and ad interaction diagnostics—to serve and measure ads.

  • Review Google's Privacy & Terms: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Manage or opt out of personalized ads: https://myadcenter.google.com/

4. Permissions Used by the App

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to load and display Google AdMob ads when an internet connection is available.
  • VIBRATE: Used for optional haptic feedback during gameplay (can be disabled anytime in Settings).
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Used by Google AdMob in accordance with Google Play policies. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android device Settings under Privacy > Ads.

5. Data Security, Retention & Deletion

All game save data resides locally on your device. You can delete all stored progress at any time via Settings > Reset Progress inside the app or by uninstalling Arrow Puzzle. Data processed by Google AdMob is subject to Google's security and retention policies.

6. Children's Privacy

Arrow Puzzle does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you are a parent or guardian and believe personal data has been collected, please contact us so we can address your concern promptly.
